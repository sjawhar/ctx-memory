# Converting a meeting

Step 4 of the ingest skill's per-cycle procedure sends you here when a window holds a
meeting: its notes, its transcript or its calendar event.

- Convert meetings into one record per meeting. Keep notes and transcript as separate
  sources, and check notes against the transcript.
- Mint or update a commitment for each action-list row that names a person. A disposition
  on the meeting record takes its place only when the row names no identifiable person, the
  item is already closed, or the source rejects it, and the disposition says which.
- For a meeting with only a transcript, read the whole transcript for assigned actions.
- Speaker labels from diarization are provisional: establish who said what from the
  attendee list and the transcript's own content, and mark attribution uncertain when the
  evidence does not settle it.
